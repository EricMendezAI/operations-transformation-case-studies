# Local Splash — Revenue Growth and Delivery Efficiency

## Context

At Local Splash, I led a seven-person cross-functional team responsible for custom website work inside a digital marketing environment.

The opportunity was not simply to "sell more." Growth depended on improving how the department operated so that increased demand could be supported without creating a delivery bottleneck.

## Operating Constraint

Revenue growth and delivery capacity were linked. If the team could not move work through development efficiently, adding more volume would create delays rather than sustainable growth.

The challenge therefore required attention to both:
- commercial performance,
- and the workflow used to deliver the work.

## Approach

I focused on improving the operating system around the team:
- clarified workflow and ownership,
- improved coordination across the people involved in delivery,
- created stronger visibility into targets and performance,
- reduced avoidable friction in the development process,
- aligned the team around both revenue and execution.

Rather than treating sales performance and delivery performance as separate problems, the work addressed them as one system.

## Measurable Outcome

- **800% increase in department revenue**
- **50% reduction in website development time**

## Why It Matters

The result illustrates an operating principle I have carried into later roles: growth is only valuable when the system can support it.

The most important improvement was not one isolated tactic. It was creating a better relationship between demand, workflow, accountability, and delivery capacity.

## What I Would Measure Today

If I were instrumenting the same type of transformation today, I would explicitly track:
- lead-to-start conversion,
- work-in-progress volume,
- cycle time by production stage,
- rework rate,
- capacity by role,
- revenue per completed project,
- on-time delivery,
- customer satisfaction.

That would make the constraint visible earlier and make future improvements easier to prioritize.
