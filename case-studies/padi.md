# PADI — Global Digital Platform Transformation

## Context

At PADI, I worked in digital performance and conversion during a period that included a major redesign of PADI.com and migration from Ektron to Drupal.

The work supported a global organization operating across markets and languages, so the transformation had to account for more than a single website launch.

## Operating Challenge

A platform migration of this scale connects multiple systems and stakeholders:
- content,
- SEO,
- analytics,
- localization,
- development,
- QA,
- business requirements,
- user experience.

The challenge was to improve the platform without losing the visibility, measurement, and localized experience required across global markets.

## Contribution

I was a major contributor to the redesign and migration, with work spanning:
- digital performance analysis,
- SEO,
- site optimization,
- analytics,
- localization support,
- implementation and QA,
- cross-functional coordination.

The rollout ultimately supported **26 languages**.

## Measurable Outcome

Across my work at PADI, digital optimization contributed to a **42% combined improvement in website traffic, search, and engagement**.

The platform transformation also created a more modern foundation for the organization's global digital presence.

## Why It Matters

Large transformations succeed when technical migration and business performance are treated as one problem.

A new platform is not valuable simply because it launches. It has to preserve or improve:
- discoverability,
- measurement,
- user experience,
- operational maintainability,
- and the ability of teams to work effectively after launch.

## Transferable Operating Lesson

For complex transformations, define success beyond "project completed."

A durable implementation should answer:
- Did the system work?
- Could teams operate it?
- Could performance still be measured?
- Did users have a better experience?
- Could the platform support future growth?

That keeps delivery focused on business outcomes rather than the launch date alone.
